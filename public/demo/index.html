A quieter kind of travel

Slow mornings, small roads, and the places we find when the itinerary leaves room for surprise.

The road out of town bends through the foothills, where late-summer grass catches the light like brushed copper.

We stopped without a plan, following a hand-painted sign toward an orchard at the end of a dusty lane. There was coffee under the sycamores and nowhere else we needed to be.

“The best part of going slowly is noticing when you have arrived.”
